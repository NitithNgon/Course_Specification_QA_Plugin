### Course description (คำบรรยายรายวิชา)

The relational model; SQL; entity-relationship modelling; functional dependencies and normalization; physical design and indexing; query processing and optimization; transactions, concurrency control and recovery; an overview of non-relational databases.

### Assessment note (หมายเหตุเกี่ยวกับการประเมินผลการเรียน)

- Deadlines: project milestones have fixed dates. A one-day grace period may be used once per semester; after that, late milestones are not accepted.
- Academic integrity: schemas, queries and reports must be your own or, for group deliverables, your group's. Copying from another student or an uncredited source, or citing a source that does not exist, is an academic-integrity violation.
- AI tools: you may use AI tools to explore alternative queries. The schema rationale must be written in your own words and defended at the project review.
- Attendance: attendance is not graded.
