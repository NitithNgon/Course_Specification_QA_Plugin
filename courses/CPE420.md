### Course description (คำบรรยายรายวิชา)

Software lifecycle models; project planning and estimation; requirements; software architecture and its documentation; agile teamwork in sprints; code review, testing and continuous integration; reporting project status; a semester-long team project with a live demonstration.

- PLO3: Design/development of solutions

### Assessment note (หมายเหตุเกี่ยวกับการประเมินผลการเรียน)

- Attendance: attendance at sprint reviews is required; three unexcused absences forfeit the sprint-review marks.
- Deadlines: a late sprint deliverable loses 15% of that sprint's marks.
- Academic integrity: contribution logs from version control are used to verify individual participation. Presenting another team's architecture or a tutorial's design as your own is a violation.
- AI tools: AI tools may draft scaffolding and documentation; architectural decisions and their justifications must be the team's own and are defended in the demonstration questions.
