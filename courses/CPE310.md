### Course description (คำบรรยายรายวิชา)

Network architecture and the TCP/IP model; application, transport, network and link layers; IP addressing, subnetting and routing; wireless networks; packet capture and network simulation; troubleshooting network faults.

- PLO1: Engineering knowledge
- PLO2: Problem analysis
- PLO5: Tool usage

### Assessment note (หมายเหตุเกี่ยวกับการประเมินผลการเรียน)

- Attendance: lab attendance is required; two unexcused absences forfeit the lab-report marks for those weeks.
- Deadlines: lab reports are due 48 hours after the lab; late reports are not accepted.
- Academic integrity: capture analyses must be your own. Submitting another student's analysis or fabricating capture data is reported to the department.
- AI tools: AI tools may explain protocol behaviour, but every diagnostic conclusion must be checked against the capture data provided.
