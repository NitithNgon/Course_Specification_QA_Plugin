# Physics Simulation for Games

Course code: CPE450

Instructor: Assoc. Prof. Dr. Preecha Srisawat

Prerequisite: Algorithm Design; no prior computer-graphics background is needed

## What the course covers

Students learn the theory and practice of programming physics simulations for games and interactive graphics applications that must compute and display their results at high speed. Each topic is taught in lectures first, and students then write a program that uses it as homework.

Topics (may change as appropriate):

- Human perception
- Review of vector mathematics; 2D, 3D and perspective transformations
- Particle simulation
- Rigid-body simulation: theory and practice
- Position-based dynamics
- Gas simulation
- Basic water simulation: height fields, shallow water and 3D grids

## Grading

- Attendance: 10%
- Homework: 20%
- Midterm exam: 30%
- Final project (individual, or groups of 3-4 students): 40%
