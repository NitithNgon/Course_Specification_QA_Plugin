### Course description (คำบรรยายรายวิชา)

Complexity analysis of algorithms; lists, stacks, queues, trees, heaps, hash tables and graphs; sorting and searching; algorithm design strategies including divide and conquer, greedy methods and dynamic programming.

## Program learning outcomes related to the course (ผลลัพธ์การเรียนรู้ระดับหลักสูตรที่เกี่ยวข้องกับรายวิชา)

- PLO1: Engineering knowledge
- PLO2: Problem analysis
- PLO3: Design/development of solutions

### Assessment note (หมายเหตุเกี่ยวกับการประเมินผลการเรียน)

- Late work: homework submitted late loses 10% of its mark per day, up to 3 days; it is not accepted after that.
- Academic integrity: all submitted work must be your own. Copying code or text from another student or an uncredited source, or citing a source that does not exist, is handled under the university's academic-integrity regulations and scores zero.
- AI tools: AI coding assistants may be used for boilerplate code only. Disclose any AI use in the submission header and be ready to explain every line you submit.
- Attendance: attendance is expected but not graded.
