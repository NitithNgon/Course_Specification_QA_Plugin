# Course Specification

## 1. Course Information
- **Course Code:** CPE310
- **Course Name:** Computer Networks

## 2. Learning Outcomes

| CLO | Description | Bloom Level | PLO |
|---|---|---|---|
| CLO1 | Identify the layers of the TCP/IP model and their responsibilities | Remember | PLO1 |
| CLO2 | Apply subnetting and routing calculations to a given network topology | Apply | PLO3 |
| CLO3 | Analyze packet captures to diagnose a network fault | Analyze | PLO2 |

| Component | Weight (%) | CLO Mapped |

| Midterm Exam | 30 | CLO1, CLO2 |
| Final Exam | 30 | CLO1, CLO2, CLO3 |
| Assignment | 25 | CLO2, CLO3 |
| Project | 15 | CLO3 |

**Total: 100**

### Scoring Criteria Checklist
- [x] Rubric published on the LMS before the due date

## 4. Course Policies
- **Attendance:** Lab attendance is mandatory; two missed labs without medical excuse forfeit the lab component.
- **Deadlines:** Lab reports are due within 48 hours of the session; late reports are not accepted.
- **Academic Integrity:** Packet-capture analysis reports must reflect the student's own diagnostic reasoning.
- **Plagiarism and False Citation:** Submitting another student's capture analysis or fabricating capture data is a violation reported to the department.
- **AI Tools & Critical Use:** AI tools may assist in explaining protocol behavior, but diagnostic conclusions in lab reports must be independently verified against the actual capture data provided.
