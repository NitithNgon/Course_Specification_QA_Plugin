### Course description (คำบรรยายรายวิชา)

Supervised learning; linear and logistic models; the bias-variance trade-off and regularization; model evaluation and cross-validation; decision trees and ensembles; neural networks; data preparation; machine-learning pipelines from training to deployment.

### Assessment note (หมายเหตุเกี่ยวกับการประเมินผลการเรียน)

- Attendance: attendance is checked at every session and is worth 5% of the grade.
- Late work: homework submitted late loses 10% of its mark per day, up to 3 days.
- Academic integrity: all submitted work must be your own. Uncredited use of another person's code or text, or fabricated citations, scores zero and is reported to the department.
- AI tools: AI coding assistants may be used for boilerplate code; disclose their use and be able to explain any generated code.
