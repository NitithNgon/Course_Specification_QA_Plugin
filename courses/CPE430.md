### Course description (คำบรรยายรายวิชา)

A presentation-based practice course on building agentic AI systems: large language models and agent loops, prompting and structured output, tool use, memory and retrieval, multi-agent orchestration and agent evaluation. Groups of three practise one topic at home, present their results in class, and finish with a team project pitched to invited guests.

### Assessment note (หมายเหตุเกี่ยวกับการประเมินผลการเรียน)

- Final project (28 points): use case 5, progress presentation 4, progress discussion 4, final report 8, final pitch 7.
- Final project at a glance: use case (5) + progress presentation (4) + progress discussion (2 × 2) + final pitch (15).
- Attendance: attendance is checked in the first 30 minutes of every session; an absent student scores zero for that session's presentation and critique notes.
- Deadlines: topic practice reports are due before class; a report submitted after class starts scores zero.
- Academic integrity: copying another group's presentation, report or notes is academic misconduct under university regulations. Plagiarism and false citation are grounds for an F in the course.
- AI tools and critical use: this course uses AI tools throughout. Disclose AI-assisted content, and verify every claim, citation and result before you submit it.
