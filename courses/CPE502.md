### Course description (คำบรรยายรายวิชา)

Human visual perception; vector mathematics and 2D, 3D and perspective transformations; the real-time rendering pipeline; programmable shaders; lighting, bump mapping, environment mapping, shadow maps and post-processing; licensing and attribution of third-party assets; an original interactive scene as the final project.

### Assessment note (หมายเหตุเกี่ยวกับการประเมินผลการเรียน)

- Class participation (5%) is graded separately by the teaching assistants and added at the end of term.
- Attendance: attendance is taken at every session and is worth 10%; missing two critique sessions forfeits half of it.
- Late work: late milestones lose 10% of their mark per day.
- Academic integrity: shader code and scene assets must be your own, or licensed and credited. Reusing another student's shader code or an uncredited asset pack is a violation.
- AI tools: disclose any AI-generated art or code; the lighting code being assessed must be your own.
